Lekki-Ajah Real Estate Location Intelligence

A geospatial decision-support prototype that combines OpenStreetMap context layers with a weighted proximity model to explore location suitability across five Lekki-Ajah sub-markets.

01 / The challenge

Look beyond a neighbourhood label

A location’s name or asking price alone does not show how close it is to roads, public transport, schools, healthcare, markets, or mapped water.

This project demonstrates a way to bring those location factors into one map and compare illustrative property scenarios. It uses proximity to mapped features as a consistent screening framework, rather than treating neighbourhood reputation or asking price as a complete picture of suitability.

03 / Workflow

From spatial layers to an interactive comparison

STEP 01

Assemble map context

Bring together OSM roads, mapped water bodies, schools, hospitals, markets and bus stops for the study area.

STEP 02

Prepare the scenario data

Create a 100-record synthetic property dataset across five sub-markets for a repeatable demonstration.

STEP 03

Measure proximity

Reproject spatial layers to EPSG:32631 and calculate straight-line distances from property points to nearby features.

STEP 04

Score and explore

Combine weighted distance bands and unit land-price criteria into a composite score, then explore the scenarios in a web dashboard.

Composite score (0–100) = Flood-risk proxy (20) + Road access (15) + Bus access (10) + School access (10) + Healthcare access (10) + Market access (10) + Unit land value (25)

The report assigns points using set distance bands—for example, proximity to water, roads, bus stops, schools, hospitals and markets—and gives unit land value a 25-point weight. Flats with no land area receive a neutral price component in the described model.

05 / Limitations

A screening prototype, not a property or flood assessment

Known limitations documented in the project report
LimitationWhy it matters
Synthetic property recordsNames, coordinates, prices and property details do not represent verified listings or a sampled real-estate market.
Distance to water is only a proxyProximity alone does not establish flood hazard. Elevation, drainage, historical flooding and engineering assessment are not included.
Coordinate drift noted in reportSome records labelled Ikate, Ajah and Sangotedo have coordinates that appear to overlap western Lekki/Lagos Island; sub-market comparisons require coordinate validation.
Straight-line distanceEuclidean proximity is not travel distance or travel time; it does not account for road-network barriers, access points or traffic.
