Spatial Distribution and Equity of Primary Health Care Facilities in Ikeja LGA

This project presents two related but distinct analyses of 108 primary healthcare facilities in Ikeja: a QGIS map counting facilities in each 500 m grid cell, and an interactive dashboard comparing population per PHC with a 10,000-person benchmark.

Open live dashboard

01 / The problem

Where does local access fall short?

Where are PHCs located, and which grid cells have the greatest population burden relative to available facilities? The project answers these with a facility-count map and a separate population-equity dashboard.

03 / Method

Two complementary analysis workflows

STEP 01

Create the grid

Generated 208 cells of 500 m × 500 m across Ikeja LGA.

STEP 02

Map facility distribution

Counted PHC points per cell. The QGIS layout map shows no facility, one facility, or 2+ facilities.

STEP 03

Analyze people served

For the dashboard analysis, used zonal statistics to summarize WorldPop population in each cell.

STEP 04

Classify dashboard equity

Separated zero-facility cells above 10,000 people from those at or below 10,000; cells with facilities were assessed by people per PHC.

people per PHC = cell population / facility count

My role: I carried out the GIS analysis in QGIS, including the facility-count map and population-equity analysis, and coded the interactive dashboard.

06 / Finding

Most cells were classified as underserved

A combined 144 of 208 cells (69.2%) were classified as Urgent Need or Underserved. The 71 Urgent Need cells have no PHC and were identified in the report as high-priority intervention areas. The 64 Adequate cells cluster around central Ikeja, GRA, and Allen Avenue, where most of the mapped facilities are located. The report found no cells classified as Overserved.

Planning relevance: The cell-level view highlights areas for closer review when planning facility placement and healthcare resources. The Urgent Need cells offer a starting point for further assessment.

07 / Data context

Sources and limitations

Data sources

  • Facility locations: Nigeria Health Facility Registry 2021
  • Population: WorldPop Nigeria 2024, 100 m resolution
  • Boundary: GRID3

What the grid shows

The static map displays facility counts per 500 m × 500 m cell. Separately, the dashboard summarizes population per cell and compares people per PHC with the 10,000-person benchmark.

Interpret with care

The grid is a local summary, not a travel-time or road-network analysis. It does not measure facility capacity, staffing, medicines, opening hours, or service quality. Facility and population data are from different reference years.

08 / Planning insight

Next step
