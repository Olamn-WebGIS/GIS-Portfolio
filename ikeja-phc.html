Primary healthcare · Ikeja LGA, Lagos State

Spatial Distribution and Equity of Primary Health Care Facilities in Ikeja LGA

A 500 m grid-based GIS analysis of 108 primary healthcare facilities and WorldPop 2024 estimates across Ikeja. The analysis classified 208 cells by facility coverage and population, while an interactive dashboard makes the equity patterns explorable.

Open live dashboard

01 / The problem

Where does local access fall short?

Facility totals alone can hide local gaps. This analysis compares PHC locations with population in small grid cells to identify where residents may face the greatest shortage of nearby services.

03 / Method

From facility points to equity classes

STEP 01

Create the grid

Generated 208 cells of 500 m × 500 m across Ikeja LGA.

STEP 02

Count facilities

Counted PHC points within each cell to identify facility availability.

STEP 03

Summarize population

Used zonal statistics to summarize WorldPop population in each cell.

STEP 04

Classify equity

Compared facility availability and people per facility with the report’s WHO benchmark.

people per PHC = cell population / facility count

My role: I carried out the GIS analysis in QGIS and coded the interactive dashboard to explore the results.

05 / Finding

Most cells were classified as underserved

A combined 144 of 208 cells (69.2%) were classified as Urgent Need or Underserved. The 71 Urgent Need cells have no PHC and were identified in the report as high-priority intervention areas. The 64 Adequate cells cluster around central Ikeja, GRA, and Allen Avenue, where most of the mapped facilities are located. The report found no cells classified as Overserved.

Planning relevance: The cell-level view highlights areas for closer review when planning facility placement and healthcare resources. The Urgent Need cells offer a starting point for further assessment.

06 / Data context

Sources and limitations

Data sources

  • Facility locations: Nigeria Health Facility Registry 2021
  • Population: WorldPop Nigeria 2024, 100 m resolution
  • Boundary: GRID3

What the grid shows

Facility counts and estimated population summarized within 500 m × 500 m cells. The report uses the WHO benchmark of one PHC per 10,000 people to assess adequacy.

Interpret with care

The grid is a local summary, not a travel-time or road-network analysis. It does not measure facility capacity, staffing, medicines, opening hours, or service quality. Facility and population data are from different reference years.

07 / Planning insight

Next step

Use the Urgent Need cells as priority areas for field validation and further assessment before selecting sites for new PHC services.
