Primary healthcare · Ondo State, Nigeria

Primary Healthcare Accessibility and Service Equity Analysis in Ondo State

Using PHC facility locations, WorldPop population data, and GIS accessibility analysis, this project evaluated whether residents across Ondo State have equitable access to primary healthcare. Results show that 17 of 18 LGAs meet the benchmark of 1 PHC per 10,000 people, while Akure South remains underserved and should be prioritized for future healthcare investments.

01 / The problem

Where are healthcare access gaps?

Access to healthcare in Ondo State is unequal, but no one had mapped it by PHC per 10,000 people. This project asks: Who is left behind?

05 / Finding

Service access across Ondo State

17 out of 18 LGAs are ABOVE the standard of 1.0 PHC per 10,000 people. The only underserved LGA is Akure South (0.87), where one PHC serves 11,446 people. Despite having 60 PHCs, Akure South remains the only LGA below the recommended threshold because its population size substantially exceeds the capacity of existing facilities. In contrast, Akoko South East (4.49) has the best access.

Planning impact: The analysis provides evidence that can support healthcare planning and resource allocation decisions by identifying underserved areas and highlighting disparities in PHC distribution across Ondo State.

06 / Data context

Data & Limitations

Data Sources

  • PHC locations: 840 points - [Nigeria health facilities]
  • Population: WorldPop 2024 - 5,255,952 people
  • Boundaries: 18 LGA boundaries - [GRID3]

What the map shows

The 5km buffers show the area within 5km of a PHC. The PHC per 10,000 metric shows availability - how many clinics exist for the population.

What it does NOT show

It does NOT measure travel time, road condition, whether the clinic has staff / drugs, or whether the clinic is actually open. A LGA can look "well-served" on this map but still have poor care if facilities are understaffed.

07 / Recommendation

Planning priority

New PHCs should be prioritised in Akure South, not in already well-served LGAs.
